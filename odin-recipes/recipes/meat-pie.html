Meat Pie


Description

Meet the latest obsession in the world of desserts!-Meat Pie! A savory pie filled with seasoned meat and vegetables. It's a perfect balance of flavors that will leave you wanting more. Serve it hot for a hearty meal or cold for a refreshing snack. Enjoy this delightful pie any time of the year!


Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/4 cup chopped celery
  • 1/4 cup chopped mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup beef broth
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 package of pie crusts

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, chopped onion, chopped bell pepper, chopped carrots, chopped celery, chopped mushrooms, tomato paste, beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, dried thyme, salt, and pepper. Mix until well combined.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Roll out one pie crust and place it in a 9-inch pie dish.
  4. Fill the crust with the meat mixture.
  5. Roll out the second pie crust and place it over the filling.
  6. Cut vents in the top crust and crimp the edges to seal.
  7. Bake for 45-50 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the filling is heated through.
  8. Let cool for a few minutes before serving. Enjoy!
