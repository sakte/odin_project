Arayes


Description

Meet the latest obsession in the world of desserts!-Arayes! Pita pockets stuffed with seasoned meat kofta filling. Then pan fried until cripsy. Think: Lebanese quesadillas/ easy gozleme. Enjoy for dinner or pass them around as an appetise. I can't stop eating these!


Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef or lamb
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped parsley
  • 1/4 cup chopped mint
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp ground coriander
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp ground allspice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 4 pita breads
  • Olive oil for brushing

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef or lamb, chopped onion, chopped parsley, chopped mint, ground cumin, ground coriander, ground cinnamon, ground allspice, salt, and pepper. Mix until well combined.
  2. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  3. Roll out the pita breads and place them on a baking sheet.
  4. Brush the pita breads with olive oil.
  5. Bake for 10-15 minutes until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Remove from the oven and let cool for a few minutes.
  7. Stuff each pita pocket with the seasoned meat kofta filling.
  8. Pan fry the stuffed pita pockets until golden brown and crispy on both sides.
  9. Serve immediately with your favorite dipping sauce. Enjoy!
