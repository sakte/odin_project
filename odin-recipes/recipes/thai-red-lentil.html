Thai Red Lentil


Description

Meet the latest obsession in the world of desserts!-Thai Red Lentil! A creamy and flavorful dish made with red lentils and Thai spices. It's a perfect balance of sweet and spicy flavors that will leave you wanting more. Serve it hot for a hearty meal or as a side dish for your guests. Enjoy this delightful dish any time of the year!


Ingredients

  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup chopped bell pepper
  • 1/4 cup chopped carrots
  • 1/4 cup chopped celery
  • 1/4 cup chopped mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup coconut milk
  • 1/4 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 tsp red curry paste
  • 1 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the red lentils and set aside.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the chopped onion, bell pepper, carrots, celery, and mushrooms until softened.
  3. Add the red curry paste, ground ginger, and ground turmeric to the pot and cook for 1-2 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add the rinsed red lentils, coconut milk, and vegetable broth to the pot. Stir to combine.
  5. Bring the mixture to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 20-25 minutes until the lentils are tender and the mixture has thickened.
  6. Season with salt and pepper to taste. Serve hot and enjoy!
